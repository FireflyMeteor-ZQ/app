技术支持

更新 2026-11-06适用版本 2.0.0（iPhone）iOS 16.6 及以上

先看这里：本 App 完全离线、没有服务器，所以「照片传丢了」「服务器处理失败」这类问题不存在。真正会遇到的问题集中在三处：订阅由 Apple 保管、相册权限、以及抠图质量与原始照片有关。下面按类别列出。

联系开发者：zjsaufe@126.com，工作日 48 小时内回复。请在邮件里写清：机型、iOS 版本、App 版本号（我的 → 页面底部）、复现步骤、是否已购买 Pro。
请不要附上你要处理的原始照片——我们看不到你的照片，也不需要它来定位问题；涉及人脸的素材也请避免通过邮件发送。

订阅、试用与付款
买了 Pro 但功能还是没解锁，怎么办？

到「我的 → 恢复购买」（或付费墙底部的「恢复购买」）点一次。购买凭证绑定 Apple ID，不绑定 App，换机或重装后必须手动恢复。若仍未解锁，等 1–2 分钟让收据校验完成，再完全退出 App 重开。

仍无效时检查两件事：当前登录的 App Store 账号是不是当时付款的那个；付款是否真的成功（apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions 能看到）。

年订阅的 3 天免费试用为什么没有生效？

试用资格由 Apple 按 Apple ID 判定，同一个订阅组一个账号只能用一次。如果这个 Apple ID 之前订阅过本 App 的周订阅或年订阅，新方案不会再带试用。付费墙是否显示「前 3 天免费」，取决于 App 从 StoreKit 读到的真实资格，而不是 App 内部的一个开关。

沙盒测试环境下的试用行为与生产环境不同，这属于正常现象。

怎么取消自动续订？

App 内没有取消按钮，订阅由 Apple 保管。请前往 iOS「设置 → Apple ID → 订阅 → 证件照大师」取消，或访问 apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions。

必须在当前周期结束前至少 24 小时取消，否则 Apple 会收取下一周期费用。取消后，已付费周期的剩余时间照常可用。

我想退款。

应用内购买的退款只由 Apple 受理：reportaproblem.apple.com → 登录 Apple ID → 找到这笔订单 → 请求退款。开发者无法代办退款，也不会因为你申请退款而限制账户。

如果是 App 本身的问题导致你想退款，欢迎同时邮件告诉我们原因。

能换方案吗？为什么价格和我看到的不一样？

周订阅与年订阅属于同一个订阅组，可以在 Apple 的订阅管理页直接升级或降级，变更在周期边界生效。终身买断是一次性商品，不自动续订；从订阅改为终身的用户，建议先取消原订阅再购买。

价格由 App Store 按你的商店地区返回，含税、按当地货币展示，所以不同地区看到的数字不同。以付费墙显示为准。

每天 3 次导出的额度怎么算？

按设备本地时间每日 0 点重置，App 内与「我的」页都能看到剩余次数。额度在点「保存」时扣减；如果相册权限被拒或保存失败，会自动退还，不会白扣。

Pro 用户不显示额度计数，导出不限次数。

相册、照片与隐私
我的照片会被上传吗？

不会。抠图、换底色、裁剪、加水印全部在你的设备上完成，App 内没有上传代码，也没有服务器。相册权限只用于读取你选中的那一张照片、保存成品。详见隐私政策。

保存到相册失败 / 提示没有权限。
  1. 到 iOS「设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 照片 → 证件照大师」，选择「所有照片」或「选中的照片」。
  2. 回到 App 重新点保存。额度会自动退还，可再次导出。
  3. 如果设备开启了「屏幕使用时间 → 内容和隐私访问限制 → 照片」，请确认没有限制新增照片。
我想把所有本地数据清掉。

「我的 → 清除本地数据」会清空语言偏好、今日导出计数与首启引导标记，操作会二次确认。此操作不影响你已购买的订阅——订阅需按上面的方式在 Apple 侧取消。

想连沙盒一起清掉，可在主屏长按 App → 移除 App → 删除 App。

抠图与构图
边缘不干净，头发周围有白边或缺口。

抠图基于统计模型，原图质量直接决定结果。最有效的四件事：

  • 用正面、光线均匀、背景与服装颜色有明显差异的照片；
  • 避免重度美颜或磨皮，发丝细节被抹掉后模型无从判断；
  • 避免逆光与大面积阴影；
  • 单人照，面部在画面中占较大比例。

App 会自动补上发丝内部与肩颈之间的空洞，如果原图中该区域与背景颜色接近，仍可能出现残留。

构图偏了：头顶留白太多或太少。

App 会用蒙版测量真实发顶，并按目标尺寸的头部比例重算裁剪框。如果原图里人物贴边、头顶被画面裁掉，测量会失真。请换一张头顶上方留有空间的照片重试。

提示「未检测到人脸 / 人脸太小 / 人脸未居中 / 多张人脸」。

这是制作前的校验，目的是避免导出废片。换一张正面、单人、面部清晰的照片即可。戴墨镜、遮挡面部、侧脸角度过大的照片通常会被判定为未检测到人脸。

输出尺寸和机构要求不一致。

每个预设按对应机构的公开规格定义宽高（毫米）与 300 DPI。不同机构（尤其使领馆）会不定期调整要求，正式提交前请以该机构最新公告为准；本 App 不保证成品一定通过任何机构的审核。

水印、界面与语言
水印保存后和预览不一样？

预览按屏幕分辨率缩放显示，保存时按原图分辨率渲染。字号是按图片宽度的百分比计算的，所以在高分辨率原图上看起来比例一致、但文字更清晰。若发现明显偏差，请把原图与成品一起描述给我们（不必发送照片本身，说明像素尺寸即可）。

怎么把界面切成英文 / 深色？

App 内「我的 → 语言设置」，可选跟随系统 / 简体中文 / English，切换后立即生效，无需重启。界面外观跟随 iOS 系统的浅色 / 深色模式，App 没有单独的外观开关。

为什么 App 是纯 iPhone 应用？

本 App 目前只针对 iPhone 设计（竖屏）。在 iPad 上以兼容模式运行可以使用，但布局按手机尺寸渲染，不保证为 iPad 优化。Mac 暂不支持。

反馈与故障
App 闪退 / 卡住 / 结果异常。

请先：更新到最新系统版本 → 完全退出 App 再打开 → 在 App Store 更新到最新版。仍复现的话，邮件告诉我们机型、iOS 版本、App 版本号、复现步骤与出现频率，有截图更好。我们看不到你的崩溃日志，描述得越具体，修得越快。

想要一个新尺寸 / 新功能 / 觉得某处文案不通顺。

欢迎直接发到 zjsaufe@126.com。双语用户如果发现某个词在英文里别扭，这类反馈对我们特别有用。

什么情况下你们帮不上忙？

涉及 Apple 侧的扣款、发票、退款、家庭共享、Apple ID 问题——这些只有 Apple 能处理，请走 reportaproblem.apple.com 或 Apple 支持。已经删除且从未备份的本地数据，任何人都无法恢复。

Support

Updated 2026-11-06For v2.0.0 (iPhone)iOS 16.6 or later

Start here: the app is fully offline with no server, so "my photo was lost in transit" or "server processing failed" cannot happen. The real issues cluster in three places: subscriptions held by Apple, photo library access, and cutout quality, which depends on your original photo. Listed by category below.

Contact the developer: zjsaufe@126.com, replies within 48 hours on working days. Please include the device model, iOS version, app version (bottom of the Me tab), steps to reproduce, and whether you have a Pro purchase.
Do not attach the photos you want to process — we cannot see your photos and do not need them to diagnose a problem. Avoid emailing images of people's faces.

Subscriptions, trials and payment
I bought Pro but the features are still locked.

Tap Restore Purchases in Me → Restore Purchases (or at the bottom of the paywall). The purchase is tied to your Apple ID, not the app, so after a device change or reinstall it must be restored once manually. If it still does not unlock, wait a minute or two for receipt validation, then fully quit and reopen the app.

Then check two things: that the App Store account signed in is the one that paid, and that the charge went through (visible at apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions).

Why didn't the 3-day trial on the yearly plan apply?

Trial eligibility is decided by Apple per Apple ID, once per subscription group. If this Apple ID has subscribed to this app's weekly or yearly plan before, no trial applies to a new plan. Whether the paywall shows "free for 3 days" depends on the real eligibility returned by StoreKit, not on a switch inside the app.

Trial behaviour differs in sandbox testing, which is expected.

How do I cancel auto-renewal?

There is no cancel button in the app; Apple holds the subscription. Go to iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → PassportCut, or apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends, otherwise Apple charges for the next period. After cancelling, the rest of the paid period stays available.

I want a refund.

In-app purchase refunds are handled only by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com → sign in → find the order → request a refund. The developer cannot issue refunds and will not restrict your account because you asked for one.

If the app itself is why you want a refund, we would appreciate hearing about it by email.

Can I switch plans? Why is the price different from what I saw?

Weekly and yearly belong to the same subscription group, so you can upgrade or downgrade directly in Apple's subscription screen; the change takes effect at the period boundary. Lifetime is a non-consumable that never renews — if you are moving from a subscription, cancel it with Apple before buying.

Prices come from the App Store for your storefront, including tax and local currency, so they differ by region. The price shown in the paywall governs.

How is the 3-exports-a-day quota counted?

It resets at local midnight; the remaining count is visible in the app and in the Me tab. It is deducted when you tap Save, and refunded automatically if photo library access is denied or saving fails.

Pro users see no counter — exports are unlimited.

Photos and privacy
Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Cutout, background replacement, cropping and watermarking all run on your device. There is no upload code and no server. Photo access is used to read the one image you select and to save the result. See the privacy policy.

Saving to the library fails or reports no permission.
  1. Go to iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → PassportCut and choose All Photos or Selected Photos.
  2. Return to the app and save again. The quota was refunded, so you can export again.
  3. If Screen Time → Content & Privacy Restrictions → Photos is enabled, make sure adding photos is not restricted.
I want to erase all local data.

Me → Clear Local Data clears the language preference, today's export count and the onboarding flag, after a confirmation. It does not affect a Pro purchase — cancel that with Apple as above.

To remove the sandbox too, long-press the app on the home screen → Remove App → Delete App.

Cutout and framing
Edges are messy — a halo or gaps around the hair.

Cutout is a statistical model, and the original photo decides the result. Four things help most:

  • a front-facing, evenly lit photo where the background clearly differs from your clothing;
  • no heavy beauty filtering — if the hair detail is smoothed away, the model has nothing to work with;
  • no backlighting or large shadows;
  • a single person, with the face filling a good share of the frame.

The app fills gaps inside hair and between the neck and shoulders automatically; if those areas are close in colour to the background, residue can remain.

The framing is off — too much or too little headroom.

The app measures the real top of the hair from the mask and recomputes the crop for the target size's head ratio. If the person is right against the edge of the frame or the top of the head is cut off in the original, the measurement is unreliable. Use a photo with space above the head.

"No face / face too small / not centred / multiple faces".

That is a pre-flight check to avoid wasting an export. Use a front-facing, single-person, clearly visible photo. Sunglasses, covered faces or steep profile angles are usually read as no face.

The output size doesn't match what an authority requires.

Each preset follows the issuing authority's published width, height (mm) and 300 DPI. Authorities — embassies especially — revise requirements from time to time. Check the latest published rules before submitting; the app cannot guarantee acceptance by any authority.

Watermark, interface and language
The saved watermark doesn't look like the preview.

The preview is scaled to screen resolution; saving renders at the original image resolution. Font size is a percentage of image width, so proportions match while text is sharper on a large original. If you see a real discrepancy, tell us the pixel dimensions of the original and result (no need to send the images).

How do I switch the interface to English or dark mode?

In the app: Me → Language, with Follow System / Chinese / English. It applies immediately, no restart. Appearance follows the iOS system light/dark setting; the app has no separate appearance switch.

Why is this an iPhone-only app?

The app is designed for iPhone in portrait. It runs on iPad in compatibility mode, but the layout is rendered for phone dimensions and is not optimised for iPad. Mac is not supported.

Feedback and bugs
The app crashes, hangs, or gives wrong results.

First: update to the latest iOS, fully quit and reopen the app, and update from the App Store. If it still reproduces, email us the device model, iOS version, app version, steps and how often it happens — a screenshot helps. We do not receive crash logs, so the more specific you are, the faster it gets fixed.

I want a new size or feature, or a wording feels off.

Email zjsaufe@126.com. Bilingual users noticing an awkward English term are especially useful feedback.

What can't you help with?

Apple-side billing, invoices, refunds, Family Sharing and Apple ID issues — only Apple can handle those; use reportaproblem.apple.com or Apple Support. Local data that was deleted and never backed up cannot be recovered by anyone.

1. 我们处理什么、不处理什么

证件照大师（英文名 PassportCut，以下称「本 App」）是一款在 iPhone 本地制作证件照与水印的工具。本政策说明开发者如何处理你的信息。

我们不处理的：你的照片内容、你的姓名、电话号码、邮箱地址、通讯录、位置、IMEI / IDFA 等设备标识符、你在 App 内输入的水印文字。

我们处理的：订阅相关的交易信息，由 Apple 与 RevenueCat 处理（见第 4 条）；以及保存在你设备本地的少量偏好设置（见第 5 条）。

2. 相册权限

本 App 申请相册访问权限，仅用于两个目的：

  • 读取你主动选中的那一张照片，用于抠图、换底色与加水印；
  • 把你确认后的成品照片写入相册。

我们不扫描、不索引、不分析相册中的其他照片或视频，也不在后台读取相册。你可以随时在 iOS「设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 照片 → 证件照大师」中把权限改为「仅选中的照片」或全部关闭；关闭后 App 仍可正常启动，只是无法选图与保存。

3. 图像处理全在本地

抠图（本地神经网络）、背景替换、构图校正、裁剪、合成与加水印全部在你设备的 App 沙盒内完成。App 内不存在任何网络上传代码，也没有用于接收照片的服务端接口。这意味着：

  • 即使设备离线，全部功能依然可用；
  • 你的照片不会出现在开发者的任何系统里，因为我们没有这样的系统；
  • 你的成品画质不因网络状况或服务器排队而下降。

4. 订阅与支付（第三方：Apple、RevenueCat）

Pro 周订阅、年订阅与终身买断通过 Apple App Store 完成，支付信息（卡号、账单地址等）由 Apple 处理，开发者无法获得。

我们使用 RevenueCat 校验购买状态、恢复购买并同步权益。RevenueCat 会接收：

  • 一个匿名的应用用户标识（App User ID）；
  • 购买与续订的交易记录（产品 ID、时间、状态）；
  • 设备平台与操作系统版本等基础信息。

RevenueCat 不会收到你的照片、姓名或你在 App 内输入的任何文字。它仅用于判断你的订阅是否有效。RevenueCat 的隐私政策见 revenuecat.com/privacy；Apple 的隐私政策见 apple.com/legal/privacy。

6. 我们不做的五件事

  • 不建账号：没有注册、登录、第三方账号体系。
  • 不收集照片：没有上传通道，也没有服务端。
  • 不接入分析 SDK：不埋点、不建用户画像、不采集 IDFA / IDFV。
  • 不投放广告：没有广告 SDK，也不向广告平台共享任何数据。
  • 不出售数据：我们没有可出售的数据。

7. 你的权利与数据删除

由于本 App 不收集可识别你个人身份的信息，通常不存在需要向开发者申请导出或删除的个人数据。若你想清除本地数据：

  • App 内「我的 → 清除本地数据」，会清空语言偏好、导出计数与首启引导标记（不会取消你的订阅）；
  • 卸载 App，iOS 会删除整个沙盒；
  • 订阅相关记录由 Apple 保管，需在 apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions 管理；如需删除 RevenueCat 侧的匿名标识，请邮件联系我们。

依据《个人信息保护法》《通用数据保护条例》（GDPR）等适用法律，你享有的查阅、更正、删除、撤回同意等权利，在本地数据处理的情形下由你直接通过上述方式行使。

8. 儿童隐私

本 App 不面向 13 岁以下儿童设计，也不会主动收集儿童的个人信息。由于 App 不收集任何可识别身份的信息，其使用对儿童不构成额外的隐私风险；但监护人仍应自行判断是否允许儿童使用相册与 iOS 设备。

9. 安全

  • 本地数据受 iOS 沙盒与设备加密（数据保护）保护；
  • 网络传输仅用于订阅校验，走 iOS 系统提供的 HTTPS；App 未使用自定义加密算法，加密出口合规声明为免检；
  • 开发者无法访问你设备上的照片或偏好数据。

10. 政策变更

我们可能更新本政策。更新后的版本会发布在本页并修改顶部的生效日期。若变更涉及新增数据收集，我们会在 App 内以显著方式提示后再生效。

11. 不同法域的补充说明

中国大陆：依据《个人信息保护法》，处理个人信息应遵循合法、正当、必要与诚信原则。本 App 仅在本地处理你的照片，不构成向开发者「提供」个人信息。订阅交易信息的处理者分别为 Apple 与 RevenueCat。

欧洲经济区 / 英国：就本 App 而言，开发者通常不是 GDPR 意义上的「控制者」，因为你提交给 App 的照片从未到达我们这里。订阅交易信息由 Apple（作为商户）与 RevenueCat（作为处理者）处理。

加利福尼亚州：本 App 不出售、不共享你的个人信息（CCPA/CPRA 定义），也不使用跨情境行为广告。

1. What we do and do not process

PassportCut (证件照大师) is an iPhone app for making ID photos and watermarks locally. This policy explains how the developer handles your information.

What we do not process: the content of your photos, your name, phone number, email address, contacts, location, device identifiers such as IMEI or IDFA, or the watermark text you type.

What we do process: subscription transaction information, handled by Apple and RevenueCat (section 4); and a small amount of preference data stored on your device (section 5).

2. Photo library access

The app requests photo library access for exactly two purposes:

  • reading the single photo you choose, for cutout, background replacement and watermarking;
  • writing your confirmed result back to the library.

We do not scan, index or analyse any other photo or video, and we never read the library in the background. You can change access at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → PassportCut, including limiting it to selected photos. Without access the app still launches; you simply cannot pick or save images.

3. Processing happens on-device

Cutout (a local neural network), background replacement, framing correction, cropping, compositing and watermarking all run inside the app sandbox on your device. There is no upload code and no server endpoint that could receive a photo. That means:

  • every feature works offline;
  • your photos never appear in any system of ours, because no such system exists;
  • output quality does not depend on network conditions or a queue.

4. Subscriptions and payment (Apple, RevenueCat)

Pro Weekly, Pro Yearly and Pro Lifetime are purchased through the Apple App Store. Payment details — card number, billing address — are handled by Apple and are never visible to the developer.

We use RevenueCat to validate purchase status, restore purchases and sync entitlements. RevenueCat receives:

  • an anonymous App User ID;
  • purchase and renewal transaction records (product ID, time, status);
  • basic device platform and OS version information.

RevenueCat does not receive your photos, your name, or any text you type in the app. It is used solely to determine whether your subscription is active. See revenuecat.com/privacy and apple.com/legal/privacy.

6. Five things we do not do

  • No accounts — no registration, no login, no third-party identity.
  • No photo collection — no upload path and no server.
  • No analytics SDK — no event tracking, no profiles, no IDFA or IDFV.
  • No advertising — no ad SDK, and nothing shared with ad networks.
  • No data sales — there is nothing to sell.

7. Your rights and deletion

Because the app collects no personally identifiable information, there is generally no personal data to export or erase from the developer. To clear local data:

  • in the app, Me → Clear Local Data removes the language preference, export count and onboarding flag (it does not cancel a subscription);
  • deleting the app removes the entire sandbox;
  • subscription records are held by Apple — manage them at apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions. To erase the anonymous identifier held by RevenueCat, email us.

Rights of access, correction, erasure and withdrawal of consent under the GDPR, PIPL and similar laws are exercised directly through the routes above, since the data is local to you.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because no identifiable information is collected, use poses no additional privacy risk to a child — but guardians should still decide whether a child may use the photo library and the device.

9. Security

  • local data is protected by the iOS sandbox and device encryption (data protection);
  • network traffic exists only for subscription validation and uses HTTPS provided by iOS; the app implements no custom cryptography and declares exempt encryption;
  • the developer cannot access photos or preferences stored on your device.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The new version will be published on this page with an updated effective date. If a change adds data collection, we will surface it in the app before it takes effect.

11. Regional notes

Mainland China: under the Personal Information Protection Law (PIPL), processing must be lawful, justified, necessary and in good faith. Photos are processed only locally and are never "provided" to the developer. Subscription transaction data is processed by Apple and RevenueCat.

EEA / UK: for this app the developer is generally not a controller under the GDPR, because photos you hand to the app never reach us. Subscription transaction data is processed by Apple (as merchant) and RevenueCat (as processor).

California: the app does not sell or share personal information as defined by the CCPA/CPRA, and does not use cross-context behavioural advertising.
